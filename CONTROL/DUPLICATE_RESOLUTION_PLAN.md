## Resolution evidence order

1. Internal identifier
2. Approval/signature status
3. Revision
4. Effective date
5. Content
6. Operational role/use
7. Parent/child references
8. Filename only as a last indicator

A filename containing “new”, “old”, “final”, “copy” or similar is not sufficient evidence of controlled status.

## Required end state

No two active controlled documents may share the same identifier and function. Distinct functions may use distinct identifiers only after the distinction has been documented.
